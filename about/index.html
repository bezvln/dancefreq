The creator

Hi, I’m Kolyana.

I got into EDM after the 2010s golden age and was inspired by my core three: Avicii, Kygo, and Martin Garrix.

Now I’m a college student trying to piece together my passion for EDM, the trends I’ve noticed on the dancefloor, and everything I keep hearing, saving, and wondering about. Like you, I’m still a superfan of DJs and electronic dance music, and I’m still figuring things out.

DanceFreq is my way of making that curiosity more tangible. Click my profile signal to follow a few moments that shaped the person behind it.

The idea behind the timeline

As a creator, here’s where the idea came from.

As a long-time fan of EDM, I wanted to create a more digestible and interactive way to keep up with the industry’s trends while exploring the impact electronic dance music has had across cultures, cities, and communities around the world. This timeline traces the steps from that curiosity to DanceFreq.
